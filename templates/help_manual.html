PRISMA Scientific Quality Appraisal & 2D Quadrants (Kitchenham 2007)

The Kitchenham et al. (2007) standardized rubric decouples semantic relevance (S_rel) from methodological rigor (S_qual). Each study is scored on six three-point questions and mapped onto the 2D Evidence Decision Plane below.

v5.17.0 -- Two-Tier Hierarchical Swarm Architecture. The fast single screener remains the default. The forensic --swarm mode dispatches four specialized skill auditors over smartly sliced paper sections: TheoryAuditor (Q1: formal formulation), OperationalAuditor (Q2: context realism), BenchmarkAuditor (Q3-Q4: baselines and statistical validity), and OpenScienceAuditor (Q5-Q6: reproducibility and limitations). Domain-agnostic canonical templates under src/prisma/skills/templates/ are compiled once per profile into _profiles/<name>/skills/ with the profile's research topic and inclusion/exclusion criteria injected. The swarm aggregates the six ternary scores into S_qual, reports the inter-auditor Fleiss kappa (kappa_qual), and persists the full critique payload to SQLite.

v5.17.1 -- UX Transparency & Force Re-Appraisal. Appraisal no longer exits silently when every candidate already carries a quality score: the engine renders an informational panel and re-displays the persisted 2D Evidence Quadrant distribution. The --force flag (or the TUI confirmation prompt) deliberately re-audits the entire candidate set and overwrites quality_score, quality_rubric_json, and evidence_quadrant in SQLite WAL.

python talos.py --appraise-quality --min-score 7.0 Appraise candidate papers (overall_score >= 7.0) and classify them into quadrants.
python talos.py --appraise-quality --min-score 7.0 --swarm Forensic four-auditor swarm appraisal with consensus synthesis and kappa_qual.
python talos.py --appraise-quality --min-score 7.0 --force Force re-appraisal of already-appraised candidates (overwrites quality_score / evidence_quadrant).
python talos.py --compile-skills --force Recompile the domain-specialized auditor skills for the active profile.
